DRAGONHEIR: SILENT GODS MARKS THIRD ANNIVERSARY WITH COMMUNITY-CREATED HERO AND ORDER VS. CHAOS SHOWDOWN

Child of Chaos makes his playable debut alongside a community-designed Dark Lady Collector’s Edition Figure and server-wide anniversary events

15th September 2026 – Proxima Beta is celebrating the third anniversary of dark fantasy strategy RPG Dragonheir: Silent Gods with a major event beginning 15th September, putting its community at the heart of the celebrations through two of the game’s most anticipated characters.

Reverser of Order · Child of Chaos makes his debut as a brand-new SP Hero after being created with direct input from Dragonheir players. The community helped determine his character design, skills and settings through votes and comments, and will finally be able to recruit their creation through the anniversary-exclusive Reversal Summon banner.

The community’s influence also moves beyond the game with the official Dark Lady Collector’s Edition Figure. Players participated in decisions surrounding the figure’s pose, settings and other details, bringing Supreme Order · Dark Lady to life as a physical collectible.

Together, the two projects create a virtual-to-physical celebration of Dragonheir’s relationship with its players: one community-created character coming to life inside the game, and another stepping beyond it.

ORDER OR CHAOS? PLAYERS CHOOSE THEIR SIDE

From 15th September, the rivalry between Child of Chaos and Dark Lady will form the foundation of a server-wide anniversary confrontation.

Players can take the Fated Path Test and align themselves with one of two opposing factions: the Order of Reversal, represented by Child of Chaos, or the Nightsworn Vanguard, represented by Dark Lady.

During Nightveil Standoff, players earn Rift Stardust for their faction across designated game modes, with the winning side receiving additional rewards. Coronation of Night will also allow players to support heroes within their faction, with the Champion and Top 3 ranked heroes receiving custom appearances.

CHILD OF CHAOS MAKES HIS DEBUT

The community-created Reverser of Order · Child of Chaos joins Dragonheir on 15th September as a new Light-element SP Hero.

Designed for flexible team building, Child of Chaos can be treated as any element and is immune to buffs, debuffs and crowd control. On the battlefield, he can periodically remove an allied debuff or enemy buff, restoring health to allies following a successful removal.

Child of Chaos will be available through the limited-time Reversal Summon banner, with a guaranteed unlock after 300 pulls. His exclusive Dragonbone Catalyst Artifact will also be available through the Artifact Shop during the event.

THIRD ANNIVERSARY EVENTS AND REWARDS

A series of anniversary activities will run throughout September and into October.

Born of Chaos begins on 15th September, bringing daily check-in rewards, story quests, Reversal Summon and the arrival of Child of Chaos.

Eve of the Rift also begins on 15th September, combining the server-wide faction confrontation with a 14-day Autumn's Bounty check-in campaign offering rewards including a Wyrmarrow Mining Warrant, the Three Ears Contract, Legendary Hero Lossenia, the Legendary Hero skin 'Midsummer Night's Dream' and more.

The celebrations kick off earlier with the Anniversary Prologue H5, running in three phases from 2nd September to 2nd October: Night of the Rift, First Glimpse of Fate (where players meet one of 18 Fate Heroes and determine their faction), and Gift of Fate — an eight-tier reward pool that includes the Dark Lady Collector's Edition Figure at the top tier. 

From 19th September, Dragon Crown Homage gives players another opportunity to obtain the Dark Lady Collector’s Edition Figure as a rare physical reward, with 300 total draws granting a choice between the figure and a Legendary Hero Option Chest.

Dedicated benefits will also welcome returning players back to Dragonheir, while Journey Together enables veteran players to form Bonds with new and returning Celebration Server players and earn shared rewards.

Dragonheir: Silent Gods’ third anniversary celebrations begin in full on 15th September, with individual activities continuing into October.

ABOUT DRAGONHEIR: SILENT GODS

Dragonheir: Silent Gods is a dark fantasy strategy RPG available on mobile and PC. Players can recruit from more than 300 heroes spanning diverse races and elements as they explore a vast magical world.

Combining classic tactical RPG strategy with auto-chess-inspired gameplay, battles challenge players to consider hero placement, skill timing and team synergy as they build their party and shape their adventure.

Dragonheir: Silent Gods has been renamed Dragonstride: Legacy Reborn in the United States. The displayed name may vary according to language settings; both names refer to the same game.

Dragonheir: Silent Gods is available on Android, iOS, Steam and Windows PC.

For more information, visit: https://dragonheir.sgrastudio.com/
